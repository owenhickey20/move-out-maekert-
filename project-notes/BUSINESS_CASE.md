## 1. The idea

- **Customer:** The target customer is STFX students who are looking for cheap furniture and students who are moving out that are looking to sell their furniture efficiently
- **Problem:** Students moving out often have trouble selling furniture that they don't have any need for. Students moving in have trouble finding good quality affordable furniture nearby. This takes away both problems and helps both groups of people.
- **Solution:** the product puts furniture and living essentials all in one place where it's easy to browse and find something that you need also, with the proximity of how close the furniture is someone could view it to see if they like it before committing
- **Revenue:** the company makes revenue by taking 5% of each sale.
